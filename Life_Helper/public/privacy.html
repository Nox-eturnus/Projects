Life Helper — Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Life Helper is a personal task and planning app built for, and used by, a single person: its developer. It has no accounts, no other users, no advertising, and no analytics.

What Life Helper stores

Your tasks, plans, and settings are stored on your own devices, in the app's local database. They are not sent to any server operated for this app, and are not shared with anyone.

Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, Life Helper requests read-only access (calendar.readonly). It cannot create, change, or delete anything in your calendar. It reads only each event's title, start and end time, and whether it is marked busy or free, for today and tomorrow, to show your day and work out how much free time you have. Attendees, descriptions, and locations are never requested.

Those reads go through a small server (a Cloudflare Worker) that the developer runs for this app alone. It passes event data straight back to your device and does not store or log it. Your devices keep a local copy of recent events so the app works offline. The access token Google issues is held only by that server, never on your devices.

Life Helper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendar data is used only to show your own day to you; it is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train any model.

Removing access

You can revoke Life Helper's calendar access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app then stops reading your calendar. Disconnecting a device in the app's Settings removes the calendar copy cached on that device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
